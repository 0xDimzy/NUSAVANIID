THE HERITAGE & TERROIR

Rooted in Volcanic Soil.
Refined by Generations of Craft.

Indonesia's fertile volcanic highlands provide the world's most privileged climate for Vanilla Planifolia and Tahitian orchids. High altitudes, mineral-dense soils, and consistent equatorial rains impart our pods with an unmistakable depth — warm, woody, balsamic, with intense natural vanillin retention.

We cultivate direct, multi-generational relationships with local smallholder farmers in East Java. By eliminating unnecessary intermediaries and ensuring ethical fair compensation, we preserve traditional sun-curing wisdom while implementing rigorous modern export standards.
